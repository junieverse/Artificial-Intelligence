⤷ ゛What is Artificial Intelligence ? ˎˊ˗

₊˚⊹"Well, it's simple!".༉

Artificial Intelligence is a technology that lets computers or machines copy human thinking, learning, and behaviour. A.I can look at data, understand human language—that's already been exposed to the world—make decisions, and act on their own. They work and collect information by looking at algorithms. Besides algorithms, A.I look at large amounts of data to find patterns so they can get better over time without being programmed for every task in the world. They are often called "The All-rounder Helper For Humanity"

⤷ ゛The Process of A.I ˎˊ˗

₊˚⊹"The timeline of A.I!".༉

The first time A.I was developed in the 1950s. Alan Turing is the one who proposed the Turing Test to measure whether a machine can replicate human-like intelligence. Not until 1956 did it have a name. The Dartmouth Conference officially named it Artificial Intelligence in 1956. In 1958, John McCarthy developed LISP (List Processing), the first programming language designed for A.I Research. Fast forward to the 2010s, the development in A.I became massive and even more complex. Not until 2016 did an artificial intelligence defeat world champion Lee Sedol in the game of Go. And in 2022, the public launch of ChatGPT brought A.I to everyday use. It has transformed from a small and simple technology into a complex technology that can generate many media.
